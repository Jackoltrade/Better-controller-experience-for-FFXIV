src/DualSenseLighting/Core/DualSenseReport.cs adapts the USB lighting byte
offsets, validity masks, player LED mask and pulse-cancellation command from:
DS4Windows/DS4Library/InputDevices/DualSenseDevice.cs
https://github.com/hbashton/DS4Windows/blob/2637673e0ecd03b042c30476224da398475d9cc7/DS4Windows/DS4Library/InputDevices/DualSenseDevice.cs

src/DualSenseLighting/Core/LightingEffects.cs adapts the six-sector hue-to-RGB
conversion from DS4Windows/DS4Control/DS4LightBar.cs (HuetoRGB).
https://github.com/hbashton/DS4Windows/blob/2637673e0ecd03b042c30476224da398475d9cc7/DS4Windows/DS4Control/DS4LightBar.cs

src/DualSenseLighting/Windows/HidDevices.cs adapts the shared HID device
enumeration approach from DS4Windows/HidLibrary/HidDevices.cs.
https://github.com/hbashton/DS4Windows/blob/2637673e0ecd03b042c30476224da398475d9cc7/DS4Windows/HidLibrary/HidDevices.cs

Changes made in this project, 2026-11-02:
- Restrict transport to shared-access USB DualSense/Edge gamepad interfaces.
- Remove all input/remapping, audio, haptics and trigger generation.
- Add foreground-only effects and a background lighting-validity guard.
- Add normalized settings, Dalamud configuration and regression checks.

The earlier local Steam Lighting Guard prototype was reused for the narrow
Dalamud WriteFile/HidD_SetOutputReport interception approach. It is not shipped
as a separate dependency and must be disabled before loading this replacement.
